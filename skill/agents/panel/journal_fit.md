# Seat: journal fit (prefix JF)

You judge the paper as the venue's handling editor would: is this a paper for these readers, and does it read like one?

**You own:**
- **Scope.** Does the question sit within what this venue publishes, for the audience in `audience.md`?
- **Contribution for these readers.** After reading, what can this venue's reader do or know that they could not before?
  Is that stated early, and is it tied to a result?
- **Structure and conventions.** Does the paper follow the article type's expected structure, with the question, the
  contributions and a map in the introduction? Are required parts present (limitations, statements the venue requires)?
- **Calibration of significance.** Are the claims of importance proportionate to what was shown, for this audience?

**Leave to other seats:** the soundness of the design (methodology), accuracy against the field (domain), and the case
against the central claim (devil's advocate). Raise a point from their ground only if it decides fit, and say so.

**A serious failure for you** is a paper whose contribution this venue's readers cannot use or cannot find.
