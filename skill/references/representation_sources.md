# Research basis and boundaries

Consulted 2026-10-26. These sources informed the representation guidance; they are not a
pre-approved bibliography for a user's manuscript. Reverify current venue rules at use time.
The project's source registry remains the authority for citations inserted into its paper.
The workflow, memory-state model and disposition template are this skill's synthesis, not
claims that a cited publication prescribes this exact system.

| Source | Principle used / scope |
|---|---|
| [Nature research figure guide](https://research-figure-guide.nature.com/) | Publisher guidance on sizing, accessibility, integrity and export. Apply its specifications only to the relevant venue. |
| [Nature final submission](https://www.nature.com/nature/for-authors/final-submission) | Physical size and typography are production constraints, not universal constants. |
| [JMLR formatting](https://jmlr.org/format/format.html) | A concrete counterexample to universal formatting: this guide requires at least 9-point figure text and captions below both figures and tables. Verify current guidance before use. |
| [Rougier, Droettboom and Bourne, 2014: Ten Simple Rules for Better Figures](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003833) | Start with audience and message; choose appropriate visual encodings and coherent typography. A design guide, not evidence that any one layout improves every paper. |
| [Weissgerber et al., 2015: Beyond Bar and Line Graphs](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.1002128) | Summary bars can conceal distributions and pairing. Show observations and study design where appropriate; do not invent unavailable raw data. |
| [Jambor and Bornhauser, 2024: Graphical abstracts](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011789) | Define the message, organize visual hierarchy, sketch and seek feedback. A graphical abstract has a narrower communication purpose than a full scientific figure. |
| [PLOS ONE submission guidelines](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/submission-guidelines) | Explicit statistical reporting, methods and data availability. Use study-appropriate sample sizes, interval definitions and analysis descriptions rather than decorative significance marks. |
| [EQUATOR reporting guideline library](https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/) | Select guidance appropriate to the study type; reporting checklists do not dictate a universal narrative order or certify research design quality. Primarily health research. |
| [GO FAIR Foundation: FAIR principles](https://www.gofair.foundation/fair-principles) | Identifiable resources, metadata, provenance, access conditions and reuse information. Accessible can involve authorization; FAIR is not equivalent to public access. |

## The supplied paper as an exemplar

The prior visual audit of the user-supplied `2026Zhu.pdf` informed several reusable patterns:
overview plus mechanism detail; aligned quantitative comparisons; qualitative evidence with
error cases; and separation of main argument from supporting detail. The skill does not need
that private PDF to operate. Its subject, figure counts, particular palette and layout are not
requirements. Critiques of its dense panels or encoding choices are design judgments from that
audit, not independent experimental findings about reader comprehension.

## What has and has not been established

Authoritative guidance motivates these choices, but does not establish that this skill revision
improves comprehension. Local compatibility tests check integration; a same-input project
demonstration shows changed planning decisions. Neither is a controlled reader study. Keep
empirical promotion gates and human review distinct from documentation and scenario checks.
