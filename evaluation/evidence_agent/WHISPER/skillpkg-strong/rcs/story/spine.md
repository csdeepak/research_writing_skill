# Paper Spine

1. **Problem.** Practitioners who need speech recognition or translation systems that work reliably outside the exact data distribution they were tuned on currently have to fine-tune a separately pretrained model on each target dataset, and even then performance often drops sharply once the input distribution shifts {C021}.
2. **Gap.** Self-supervised pretraining methods learn strong audio representations but still need a fine-tuned decoder before they produce usable text {E040, E041}, and models optimized for one benchmark's distribution generalize poorly to others, producing a large human-machine gap that may reflect confounding in-distribution and out-of-distribution testing rather than a genuine capability gap {C017}.
3. **Question.** Can scaling weakly supervised training data alone, with no self-supervised pretraining or per-dataset fine-tuning, produce a single speech recognition/translation model that generalizes robustly, zero-shot, across many datasets, languages, and acoustic conditions {C001}?
4. **Approach.** Train one encoder-decoder Transformer, Whisper, end-to-end on 680,000 hours of audio paired with transcripts and translations scraped from the internet across 96 languages {C001, C020}, and evaluate it zero-shot — without using any target dataset's training data — across a broad suite of English, multilingual, and translation benchmarks.
5. **Key finding.** Matched to a supervised model on LibriSpeech, zero-shot Whisper Large V2 makes 55.2% fewer errors on average across 12 other datasets (WER 29.3 → 12.8) {C002}, its long-form English transcription is close to professional human accuracy {C013}, and it sets a new state-of-the-art 29.1 BLEU on CoVoST2 X→English translation {C004}.
6. **Meaning.** Simple scale of weakly supervised data, not self-supervised representation learning or fine-tuning, is sufficient to substantially close the robustness gap between machine and human speech recognition {C016}.
7. **Main limit.** Returns diminish sharply beyond roughly 54,000 hours for English (the full 680,000-hour dataset adds only about one further WER point) {C014}, all evaluations are zero-shot with no per-dataset fine-tuning tested {L004}, and the training data remains English-heavy, so multilingual and lower-resource languages (VoxPopuli, Fleurs language ID, high-resource CoVoST2) still trail supervised systems and every reported number is a single run with no variance estimate {L001, L002, L003}.
