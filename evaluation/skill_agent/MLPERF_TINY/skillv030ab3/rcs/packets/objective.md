In the authors' words, this paper is meant to present MLPerf Tiny, an open-source benchmark suite for comparing the accuracy, latency and energy of machine-learning inference on ultra-low-power devices, motivated by the lack of a widely accepted, easily reproducible benchmark for such systems. It asks how such a benchmark can be built so that very different hardware and software stacks can be compared fairly while different kinds of contribution can still be shown, and reports what the benchmark specifies (four tasks with reference models and quality targets, closed and open divisions, a measurement procedure and framework), how the reference models compare with their targets, and what the first round of outside submissions looked like. A reader should come away understanding what the benchmark is and how it works, why the authors made each design choice, what the reported results do and do not establish, and what the authors say the benchmark does not yet capture.
