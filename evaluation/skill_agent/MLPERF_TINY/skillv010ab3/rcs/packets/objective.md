In the authors' words, this paper is meant to present MLPerf Tiny, an open-source benchmark suite for ultra-low-power machine learning systems: the problem of comparing accuracy, latency and energy across very different hardware and software stacks, what the benchmark specifies (four tasks, closed and open divisions, a measurement framework), and what its first submission round showed. A reader should come away understanding what the benchmark is, why it is designed as it is, what the first round did and did not show, and what remains uncertain.
