In the authors' words, this paper is meant to present a benchmark for testing information retrieval models zero-shot, that is on datasets and tasks they were not trained on. It asks whether performance on a model's own training dataset predicts performance elsewhere, which retrieval architectures hold up across many different datasets and at what computational cost, and whether the relevance labels of existing datasets treat systems that differ from the labelling systems fairly. A reader should come away understanding what the benchmark contains, how ten systems compared on it, what the results do and do not establish, and where the authors say the work is limited.
