Intended readers: machine-learning researchers from subfields other than information retrieval.
They can be assumed to know: general machine learning, deep learning (transformers, fine-tuning, embeddings), standard evaluation practice and statistics (variance, confidence intervals, significance tests), GPU/CPU latency.
They cannot be assumed to know: information-retrieval terminology (query, corpus, relevance judgements, nDCG, recall at k), BM25 and keyword retrieval, the specific retrieval datasets and earlier benchmarks, sparse and late-interaction retrieval variants, how test collections are pooled and labelled.
Binding reader types for this review: A (specialist in this subfield, checks precision), B (adjacent-field researcher, the main reader), E (mixed readers).
Venue type: journal-style research article (generic).
